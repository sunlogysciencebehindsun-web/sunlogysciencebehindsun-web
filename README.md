# ☀️ Sunlogy

### *Science Behind Sun*

Sunlogy is an education and awareness platform focused on helping people
understand the science of sunlight, ultraviolet (UV) radiation, skin
health, and sun protection.

🌐 **Website:** https://sunlogy.org/

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## About Sunlogy

We spend time in the sun every day, but many people do not fully
understand how ultraviolet radiation affects the skin or how
sun-protection practices can reduce exposure.

**Sunlogy exists to bridge that knowledge gap.**

The platform turns complex scientific concepts into accessible,
practical educational content so people can better understand:

-   ☀️ Sunlight and ultraviolet radiation
-   🧬 UVA, UVB, and UVC
-   🧴 Sunscreen and SPF
-   🛡️ Sun-protection practices
-   🧑‍🔬 The science behind skin exposure and photoaging
-   📚 Sunscreen testing, standards, and regulatory frameworks
-   ❌ Common misconceptions about sun exposure and sunscreen

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Our Mission

> **To educate and inspire people to understand the effects of sun
> exposure, build healthy sun-safe habits, and make informed choices
> through accessible, science-based education.**

Sunlogy focuses on **education first** --- making scientific information
understandable without removing the science behind it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What We Cover

### ☀️ Understanding the Sun

Learn how ultraviolet radiation reaches Earth and how different
wavelengths interact with the skin.

Sunlogy explains:

-   **UVA (320--400 nm)** --- associated with deeper skin penetration,
    pigmentation, photoaging, and cumulative UV exposure.
-   **UVB (280--320 nm)** --- strongly associated with sunburn and
    direct UV-related cellular damage.
-   **UVC (100--280 nm)** --- largely filtered by Earth's atmosphere
    under normal environmental conditions.

### 🧴 Understanding Sunscreen

Sunlogy explains the science behind sunscreen, including:

-   SPF and UVB protection
-   Broad-spectrum protection
-   Chemical UV filters
-   Mineral UV filters
-   Sunscreen application
-   Reapplication
-   Water resistance
-   Sunscreen testing and standards

### 🧬 Sun Exposure & Skin Health

The platform explores how UV exposure can contribute to:

-   Sunburn
-   Pigmentation
-   Photoaging
-   Changes in skin structure
-   Cumulative cellular and DNA damage

### 📊 Sunscreen Standards & Regulations

Sunlogy also provides educational comparisons of sunscreen testing and
regulatory frameworks, including international standards and frameworks
used in regions such as India and Australia/New Zealand.

## Our Approach

### 🎓 Education First

Scientific information should be understandable and useful. Sunlogy
simplifies complex concepts while keeping the underlying science
visible.

## Why Sunlogy?

Sunlogy is built around a simple idea:

> **Better awareness starts with better understanding.**

Instead of treating sunscreen and sun safety as a collection of rules,
Sunlogy explains the science behind them --- helping people understand
**why** sun protection matters.
